Mathematical & Financial Formula Directory

Transparent mathematical formulas, compounding models, and conversion algorithms used across all engines.

Banking

Recurring Deposit (RD) Compounding Formula

M = R × [ (1 + i)n - 1 ] ÷ [ 1 - (1 + i)-1/3 ]
  • M = Maturity Value
  • R = Monthly Deposit (e.g., ₹3,880)
  • i = Quarterly interest rate = Annual Rate % ÷ 400 (e.g. 6.9 ÷ 400 = 0.01725)
  • n = Total quarters = Tenure Months ÷ 3 (e.g. 24 ÷ 3 = 8 quarters)
  • Compounding: Quarterly, matching Indian Banks and Groww RD standards.

Investments

SIP (Systematic Investment Plan) Annuity Due Formula

M = P × [ ( (1 + r)n - 1 ) ÷ r ] × (1 + r)
  • P = Monthly SIP installment
  • r = Monthly return rate = Expected Annual Rate % ÷ 1200
  • n = Total number of installments (Months)
  • (1 + r) Factor: Multiplied because installments occur at the beginning of each month (annuity due).
Loans

Amortization EMI Formula (Equated Monthly Installment)

E = P × r × [ (1 + r)n ÷ ( (1 + r)n - 1 ) ]
  • P = Net Loan Principal = Asset Price - Downpayment - Exchange Value
  • r = Interest rate per installment period (e.g. Annual % ÷ 1200 for monthly)
  • n = Total installment periods (e.g. Years × 12)
  • Prepayment: Recalculates remaining tenure or reduced EMI based on selected prepayment mode.
Physics

Weight ⟷ Volume Density Conversion

Volume (L) = Mass (kg) ÷ Density (kg/L)   |   Mass (kg) = Volume (L) × Density (kg/L)
  • Water: Density = 1.000 kg/L → 1 kg = 1.000 Litre
  • Cooking Oil: Density = 0.920 kg/L → 1 kg = 1.087 Litre (1 Litre = 0.920 kg)
  • Milk: Density = 1.030 kg/L → 1 kg = 0.971 Litre (1 Litre = 1.030 kg)
  • Desi Ghee: Density = 0.905 kg/L → 1 kg = 1.105 Litre
  • Honey: Density = 1.420 kg/L → 1 kg = 0.704 Litre
